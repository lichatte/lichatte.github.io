Eli Holt

Photoshop Character Design Turnaround

Eli Holt - Midwest Character

Eli Holt is a lonely, quiet and melancholic cowboy that after enduring some very difficult events in his life, all that matters to him is his only faithful friend Dusty.

This was a project I worked on during my most recent Concept Art course taught by MST Design Academy, the brief was for a videogame character design and turnaround in a slightly cartoonish style targeted to Red Dead Redemption fans, starting with miniatures and local values for explorations.

Nidon

Photoshop Character Design Hard Surface Transportation

Nidon - Fast Food Dron

Nidon is here! He's a friendly delivery drone who'll always make sure you're staying fresh with your favorite cold beverage ~

This concept was also developed during my Concept Art course where we worked on a hard-surface cartoon mobile character based on drones and fast food delivery.

The process started with thumbnails, very focused on the main form of the drone which needed to convey very strongly the food it delivers, and added some basic notes for its functionality.

Traveler Painter

Photoshop Character Design

Traveler Painter

An old lady who knows that age is just an abstract concept and doesn\'t need anyone telling her what she can and cannot do, so she\'s taken upon herself to see all the best scenarios and preserve them for life in a canvas.

This character came up merging the painter and adventurer stereotypes, as well as adding some edge and personality with her age. I started with thumbnails, and the approach was something simple for cartoon animation.
