Facial Muscles Study

Study Character Design Facial Expressions Human Anatomy Sketchbook

Facial Expressions and Muscles Study

For character design I was taught it is very important to understand the human anatomy, once it's learned we can modify and make our own rules as we please to break the mold and create more interesting characters, as well as being able to make face gestures more articulately to communicate personalities and emotiones.

As an exercise this is something I've always loved to do on my free time, I take it as a very first step sometimes for character conceptualization and I take it as a challenge to see how many variations I can come up with during a brainstorming sesh.

Día de Muertos Site

CSS3 CSS3 Animations SVG Design

Día de Muertos Site

This was an incredibly fun project I planned with a colleague from my previous job. I focused primarily on the website design, the overall concept of its functionality, and added CSS animations for specific elements; my colleague brought it to life.

The website was presented as part of a cultural awareness meeting organized by the company. The idea was to share the essence of the Día de Muertos (Day of the Dead), not only as a holiday, but also its significance for the Latino community, the importance and purpose of each element placed on the altar, all in an interactive way.

We shared a QR code so all our colleagues could interact with the website directly and explore it by themselves.
